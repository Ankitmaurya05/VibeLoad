Respect copyright

VibeLoad does not host, index, or store user-submitted media. The tool attempts to access a direct URL in the visitor's browser. Users must only save material they own, are authorized to download, or may legally use.

Rights holder requests

If you believe VibeLoad's own website content infringes your rights, contact the site operator with the specific page URL, a description of the protected work, your contact details, and a statement explaining your rights. Replace hello@YOUR-DOMAIN.example with a monitored rights-contact address before launch.

Third-party content

VibeLoad does not control third-party hosts. For media hosted by another service, contact that service or the uploader directly. VibeLoad does not remove protections or make third-party content available for download.
