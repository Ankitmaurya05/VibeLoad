Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Information processed by the tool

VibeLoad is designed to process pasted links in your browser. The application does not send pasted URLs to a VibeLoad application server. When you choose to download a direct file, your browser connects to the media host you entered. That host may receive your IP address and normal request metadata under its own privacy policy.

Hosting and security logs

The hosting provider may process standard technical logs such as IP address, request time, user agent and requested path for security, reliability and abuse prevention. Configure your selected host's retention and privacy settings before launch.

Advertising and cookies

VibeLoad may display third-party advertising after the site owner configures an advertising account. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies subject to their own policies and applicable law. Before enabling personalized advertising, implement an appropriate consent management platform for regions where consent is required and provide the required privacy disclosures.

Children

VibeLoad is a general-purpose utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not use advertising or analytics settings that are inconsistent with applicable child privacy rules.
