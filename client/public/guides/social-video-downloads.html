Saving social media videos: use the official options

Social platforms do not all expose a direct video file URL. Their built-in save, export, and creator download options are the safest way to keep content you own or are authorized to use.

Why social links behave differently

A post URL usually opens a webpage, not a standalone MP4. The player may request multiple stream segments, use temporary signed URLs, require a logged-in session, or apply content protections. A generic downloader cannot safely assume that every public-looking page is a downloadable file.

Use official download and export tools

  • YouTube: use YouTube's available offline features or download your own uploaded videos through YouTube Studio / Google Takeout where supported.
  • Instagram: use Instagram's in-app save options and account information download for your own content.
  • TikTok: use Save video when the creator has enabled it, or export your own content.
  • Facebook: use the platform's available download or account export features for your own content.
  • Vimeo: download a video when its owner has enabled the download option.

What VibeLoad does with social links

VibeLoad identifies several common social platform URLs and explains the official next step. It does not log into accounts, scrape private content, defeat DRM, remove restrictions, or download media against a platform's rules.

Respect creators

Only save or reuse content when you own it, have permission, or have another valid legal basis. A publicly viewable post is not automatically free to copy, republish, or monetize.
